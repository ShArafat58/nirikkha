# Nirikkha Knowledge Base & Product Guide

Nirikkha (নিরীক্ষা) — "Trust less, Verify more."
Governance Crash-Test AI for corporate due diligence and critical decisions.

---

## 1. What Nirikkha Does

Before a company or investment team makes a critical decision (such as a deposit partnership, M&A, vendor agreement, investment, or credit facility), Nirikkha tests the governance evidence behind it.
It automatically ingests public SEC filings (Form 10-K annual reports and DEF 14A proxy statements), runs four parallel investigations, verifies findings through a Reliability Sentinel, and produces an evidence-linked Governance Crash-Test Report with an explainable Health Score (0–100).

### Core Principles
- **Evidence-First**: Every finding is accompanied by verifiable citations: document type, SEC accession number, filing date, section, page number, and exact supporting passage.
- **Responsible Uncertainty**: When public evidence is missing or ambiguous, Nirikkha outputs "Insufficient Evidence" rather than guessing or hallucinating.
- **Separation of Confidence and Severity**: Evidence Confidence (strength of public citation) and Risk Severity (potential operational or financial impact) are strictly independent metrics. Low evidence never lowers severity to "low risk".
- **Neutral Language**: Nirikkha never accuses a company or executive of illegal misconduct. Relationships are framed neutrally as "potential conflicts requiring review".
- **Regulatory Grounding**: Differentiates between hard law (e.g., SEC Regulation S-K rules), corporate best practices, and internal risk indicators.
- **Legal and Financial Advice Disclaimer**: Nirikkha is an analytical tool for corporate governance due diligence based on public disclosures. It does NOT provide legal, financial, or investment advice.

## 2. The Four Parallel Investigations

1. **Relationship Investigation**:
   - Analyzes directors, executive officers, committee memberships, and affiliated entities.
   - Identifies outside corporate roles and shared entities (interlocks) that may indicate undisclosed conflicts of interest under SEC Regulation S-K Item 404 (Related-Person Transactions).
2. **Cross-Document Consistency Investigation**:
   - Compares disclosures across multiple years (Form 10-K year-over-year) and between different filing types (10-K vs. DEF 14A).
   - Flags sudden omissions, altered risk wording, or contradictions between financial notes and governance statements.
3. **Regulatory Rule Check Investigation**:
   - Executes deterministic rule checks against SEC Regulation S-K requirements:
     - **Item 401**: Directors, executive officers, promoters, and control persons.
     - **Item 402**: Executive and director compensation.
     - **Item 404**: Transactions with related persons, promoters, and certain control persons.
     - **Item 407**: Corporate governance (board independence, committee charters, audit committee financial expert).
4. **Missing Evidence Investigation**:
   - Detects required governance facts that are absent or incomplete in public disclosures, highlighting blind spots where internal due diligence is required.

### Reliability Sentinel
Every finding generated by the investigations is evaluated by the Reliability Sentinel to verify:
- Does the cited passage actually support the claim?
- Is there any contradiction in the source text?
- Is the source authoritative and from the correct reporting period?
- If evidence is weak or disputed, the Sentinel marks the finding for human review (`human_review: true`).

## 3. Product Pages and Step-by-Step Usage

### Crash-Test Page (`/crash-test`)
- **Purpose**: Start a new governance analysis or explore existing evaluations.
- **Step-by-Step How-To**:
  1. Select a company from the dropdown or enter an SEC CIK number (e.g., `719739` for SVB Financial Group, `320193` for Apple).
  2. Set an optional **Evidence Freeze Date** (`as_of`). Any filing submitted after this date will be hidden from the analysis.
  3. Enter your **Decision Text** (up to 1,500 characters), describing the business context (e.g., "Evaluating a $50M credit line and treasury partnership; check risk committee oversight and related-party exposures.").
  4. Click **Run Crash-Test** (or click a precomputed demo button for instant results).
  5. Watch the 13-stage pipeline execute in real time (`redact` -> `guard` -> `collect` -> `plan` -> `gather` -> `analyze` -> `relationship` -> `consistency` -> `regulatory` -> `missing_evidence` -> `merge` -> `sentinel` -> `score`).

### Reports Page (`/reports` & `/report/[id]`)
- **Purpose**: Review complete evidence reports.
- **Features**:
  - **Health Score Banner**: Overall score, letter band (A to F), coverage percentage, and deduction details.
  - **Investigation Tabs**: Switch between Relationship, Consistency, Regulatory, and Missing Evidence findings.
  - **Finding Cards**: Shows claim, severity badge, confidence badge, mitigation recommendations, and expandable SEC citation cards with direct quotes and page numbers.
  - **Bengali Summary**: Native Bengali briefing for cross-border teams.
  - **Export**: Generate PDF or Markdown legal memos.

### Legal Memo Page (`/report/[id]/memo`)
- **Purpose**: Generates an approval-ready legal memorandum summarizing non-obvious governance risks, relevant statutory provisions, and actionable mitigation steps.
- **How-To**: From any report, click "Generate Legal Memo" or view the cached memo.

### First Warning Sign Replay (`/replay`)
- **Purpose**: Signature historical stress-test showing how Nirikkha would have flagged warning signs prior to a major corporate collapse.
- **Signature Case (Silicon Valley Bank - SVB Financial Group)**:
  - **Freeze Date**: March 7, 2023 (the day before SVB announced its capital raise and securities loss).
  - **Hindsight Guard**: Enforces strict evidence cutoff; no crisis news or FDIC receivership documents are allowed into the analysis.
  - **Replay Outcome**: Out of 5 knowable warning signs, Nirikkha detected 2 (interest rate exposure, Chief Risk Officer vacancy) and questioned 3 (deposit concentration, board oversight effectiveness, incentive compensation). 0 knowable warning signs were missed. (1 supervisory finding was confidential and not knowable from public filings).
  - **Verdict**: `VALID`.

### Relationship Graph (`/graph`)
- **Purpose**: Interactive Neo4j visualization of people, committees, and organizations.
- **Features**:
  - Visual nodes: Directors/Officers (Person), Board Committees, and Outside Organizations.
  - Interlocks: Highlights external organizations shared by two or more corporate insiders.
  - Click nodes to view associated biographical excerpts and source citations.

### Regulatory Change Radar (`/radar`)
- **Purpose**: Continuous compliance monitoring and regulatory change management.
- **Two Alert Streams**:
  1. *Curated SEC Rule Updates*: Changes to Regulation S-K and SEC disclosure guidance.
  2. *Watched Company Filings*: New Form 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and DEF 14A filings.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Decisions**:
  - Alerts are prioritized as High, Medium, or Low.
  - Reviewers can click **Approve** (to schedule follow-up diligence) or **Reject** (to dismiss).
  - Every action is recorded in the immutable `radar_decisions` audit trail.

## 4. Understanding Results and Metrics

### Governance Health Score (0–100)
- **Score Bands**:
  - 85–100: **Low Risk** (Well-documented governance, few rule gaps)
  - 70–84: **Moderate Risk** (Some omissions or potential conflicts requiring review)
  - 50–69: **Elevated Risk** (Substantial governance gaps or risk disclosure inconsistencies)
  - Below 50: **High Risk** (Critical rule failures or severe governance warning signs)
- **Coverage Metric**: Indicates the percentage of applicable rules and questions answered with sufficient evidence.
- **Insufficient Evidence Notice**: The number of unresolved evidence items is displayed transparently alongside the score.

### Rule Engine Statuses
- **PASS**: Disclosure satisfies SEC requirement with verifiable citations.
- **POTENTIAL_GAP**: Disclosure is incomplete, ambiguous, or lacks recommended specificity.
- **FAIL**: Disclosure violates specific statutory criteria or reveals a direct governance failure.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE**: Filing does not contain sufficient factual data to evaluate the rule.

### Severity vs. Confidence Matrix
- **Risk Severity**: `Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`. (Represents potential organizational risk).
- **Evidence Confidence**: `High`, `Medium`, `Low`, `Insufficient`. (Represents clarity and directness of public proof).
- Both fields are always displayed separately. A finding with high severity and low confidence means "a potentially serious issue that lacks conclusive public proof and requires direct inquiry."

## 5. Troubleshooting & Common Problems

### "Report is still loading or stuck in queue"
- On the free-tier demo environment, a live Crash-Test takes 1 to 3 minutes because it runs 4 deep investigations and Sentinel verification.
- If no background worker is online, jobs poll for up to 5 minutes before timing out.
- **Fix**: Check precomputed demo reports (e.g., SVB Financial Group) for instant walkthroughs, or wait for the current analysis run to finish.

### "Server took too long to answer / Backend waking up"
- The free cloud backend on Render enters sleep mode when idle.
- **Fix**: The first request may take 45–60 seconds to wake the server. Reload or wait a moment; once awake, responses are fast.

### "Login or authentication issues"
- You can explore the entire platform without signing up by clicking **"Try Demo"** on the login page.
- If you forgot your password, use the **Forgot Password** link to answer your security question and reset your credentials.

### "Relationship Graph appears empty"
- The graph requires parsed proxy statements (DEF 14A) with extracted executive and committee entities.
- **Fix**: Select SVB Financial Group (`719739`), which has a precomputed relationship graph with over 100 entity relationships.

### "Language Toggle: How to read in Bengali?"
- Click the language toggle button (`EN` / `বাংলা`) in the top navigation bar.
- The interface and generated findings summaries will switch into native Bengali script.

## 6. Limitations & Boundaries

1. **Public Filings Only**: Nirikkha analyzes public SEC EDGAR disclosures. Non-public information, private banking supervisory exams (e.g., CAMELS ratings), and internal board minutes are not visible.
2. **Hindsight Guard**: Replays strictly exclude post-freeze events to prevent retrospective bias.
3. **No Accusations**: The tool identifies governance gaps and conflict risks; it does not adjudicate legal guilt.
4. **Custom Integrations**: Enterprise users requiring private document ingestion or on-premise LLMs should contact the team via "Request a Pilot" on the Pricing page or review `/docs`.
